About Skolify

Skolify is a website created to help matric learners prepare for their future after school.

We understand that choosing a university and a course can be difficult. Skolify brings useful information together to make the process easier.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help matric learners find simple, useful and easy-to-understand information about universities and courses.

Our Vision

Our vision is to help learners make better decisions about their education and future careers.

Who We Help

Skolify is mainly designed for Grade 12 learners who are preparing to apply for university.

Parents and guardians can also use Skolify to find useful information about university applications.
