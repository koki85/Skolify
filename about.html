Who We Are

Skolify is a dedicated portal established to empower South African matriculants with the accurate institutional data, application parameters, and bursary resources required to make informed transitions into higher education streams.

Our goal is to make planning for life after matric clearer by bringing university options, course information, and funding guidance together in one place. Students can explore possibilities and take the next step with greater confidence.
